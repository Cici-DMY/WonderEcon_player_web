WonderEcon

An interactive economic world where your decisions shape the life of a household. Please review the informed consent below before you begin.

Informed Consent

Please read the following information carefully before deciding whether to participate.

1. Purpose of the Study

WonderEcon is a research-oriented economic simulation. The goal is to understand how individuals make economic decisions — across borrowing, employment, consumption, saving, and investment — and how a personal economic profile emerges from these decisions over time.

2. What You Will Do

You will create an economic identity (a household character) and complete a series of decision tasks in a virtual city. A typical session lasts about 15–25 minutes.

3. Data We Collect

  • The economic decisions you make and their simulated outcomes.
  • Computed economic-personality indicators (for example, risk appetite and time preference), derived from your decisions.
  • Your email address — only if you choose to provide it using the Contact us form below.

4. Risks and Benefits

Participation involves no foreseeable risks beyond those of ordinary computer use. There are no direct personal benefits, though you may find the experience engaging and educational.

5. Confidentiality

Your decisions are stored anonymously and linked only to a randomly generated participant identifier. Results are reported in aggregate and never identify you personally. If you provide an email address, it is stored separately from your gameplay data and used only to send you your results.

6. Voluntary Participation

Participation is entirely voluntary. You may stop at any time without penalty by closing the page. Data collected up to that point may still be used in anonymized form.

7. Contact

If you have any questions about the study, please reach out to the research team using the Contact us form at the bottom of this page.
